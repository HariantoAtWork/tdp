# Show current or last-set TDP step

`tdp` / `tdp show` now always reports which ladder step is active when possible.

- Prefer a live SMU read (sysfs or passwordless/`root` `ryzenadj -i`).
- Fall back to the step last written by `tdp set` in `/var/lib/tdp/step`.
- Highlight that step with `▸` and label the status as live or last-set.

This makes the default `tdp` invocation useful even when `ryzen_smu` metrics
are missing on the machine.
